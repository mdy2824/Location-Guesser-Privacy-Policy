Privacy Policy for LocationGuesser

Last Updated: September 20, 2026 • Effective Date: September 20, 2026
Transparency Summary: LocationGuesser is an offline-oriented geographic trivia game. We do not require accounts, we do not track your real-time GPS physical location, and we do not collect, sell, or monetize any personal identification information (PII).

1. Overview and Purpose

This Privacy Policy describes how LocationGuesser ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our mobile application available on Google Play. LocationGuesser is designed to test players' geographic deduction skills by presenting panoramic Street View imagery of world locations and allowing players to guess where that image was taken by placing a pin on an interactive map.

2. Information We Collect (and Do Not Collect)

We believe in absolute data minimization. LocationGuesser does not collect any personal information beyond what is strictly necessary to deliver the game's core interactive experience:

  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII) — NOT COLLECTED: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, contacts, passwords, or personal identity markers. You do not need to create an account or sign in to play.
  • Your Device's Physical GPS Location — NOT ACCESSED: The Application does not request, track, or access your real-world GPS coordinates or background device location. The only location coordinates processed are the game's pre-configured landmark locations and the virtual coordinate points you voluntarily tap on the guess map.
  • Session Gameplay State: Active game state (such as the current round number, running score, and the latitude/longitude of your guess pins) is maintained strictly in temporary device memory (RAM) while the app is active. When you restart the game or close the application, this transient data is cleared.

3. How Gameplay Coordinates Are Used

LocationGuesser processes two types of geographic coordinates strictly to support gameplay functionality:

  • Curated Target Coordinates: Pre-defined latitude and longitude coordinates of iconic worldwide landmarks are used to request panorama imagery from the Google Street View Static API so you can view visual clues.
  • User Guess Pin Coordinates: When you tap on the map modal to submit your guess, that specific map coordinate is used locally by our on-device algorithm (the Haversine formula) to calculate the distance between your guess and the target landmark in kilometers and determine your score. This calculation runs locally on your device.

4. Third-Party Services and APIs

The Application incorporates third-party developer libraries and cloud services to display maps, render street panoramas, and support app stability. These providers may receive certain technical data (such as your IP address and standard device metadata) as necessary to respond to network requests:

Google Maps Platform

LocationGuesser uses Google Maps Platform APIs, specifically:

  • Google Maps SDK for Android / Maps JavaScript API: Renders the interactive map interface where you view continents, zoom in/out, and tap to place your guess pin.
  • Google Street View Static API: Retrieves street-level panoramic images corresponding to the curated landmark rounds.

These services are governed by Google's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. When requests are made to fetch map tiles or Street View imagery, Google may log standard request information in accordance with their privacy practices.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and Google Maps Platform Terms of Service.

Firebase & Expo Framework

The Application is built using the Expo (React Native) ecosystem and may utilize core platform services (such as Firebase / Expo runtime tooling) for basic error monitoring, dependency resolution, and crash reporting. Any technical diagnostics collected by these frameworks are pseudonymous and used exclusively to diagnose application crashes and ensure compatibility across Android devices.

Learn more: Firebase Privacy and Security and Expo Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention and Storage

LocationGuesser does not operate a remote server or user database to store your personal details. Gameplay progress, scores, and round history exist solely within your local session memory. Because we do not store personal data on remote developer servers, there is no personal data retained or shared with marketing companies, data brokers, or advertising networks.

6. User Rights and Data Control

Under applicable privacy laws (including GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and Google Play User Data policies), you have the right to control your data. Because LocationGuesser does not collect or store personal identifiers:

  • You can clear all local app cache and temporary data at any time via your Android device settings: Settings → Apps → LocationGuesser → Storage & cache → Clear Storage.
  • You can remove all associated local files immediately by uninstalling the app.

7. Children's Privacy

LocationGuesser is a general audience educational trivia game. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 in certain jurisdictions). If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate measures.

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our gameplay features, API requirements, or relevant legal regulations. Any revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
