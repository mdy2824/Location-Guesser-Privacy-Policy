Privacy Policy for LocationGuesser

Last Updated: September 21, 2026 • Effective Date: September 21, 2026
Transparency Summary: LocationGuesser does not require a traditional account, email, or password. On first launch, the app generates a random device identifier and asks you to choose a username, which is required to play and is displayed publicly on the leaderboard alongside your score. We do not track your real-time GPS physical location, and we do not sell or monetize any personal information.

1. Overview and Purpose

This Privacy Policy describes how LocationGuesser ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our mobile application available on Google Play. LocationGuesser is a geography game that tests players' deduction skills through Street View panorama location-guessing rounds and image-based puzzles, with scores tracked on a public leaderboard.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum information necessary to provide the app's core features, including the persistent leaderboard:

  • Device Identifier — COLLECTED: On first launch, the app generates a random, app-specific device identifier (a UUID) and stores it on your device. This identifier is used solely to recognize your device on return visits so your username and score persist across sessions. It is not derived from your phone number, advertising ID, or any other identifier tied to your real-world identity, and it cannot be used to identify you personally on its own.
  • Username — REQUIRED TO PLAY: On first launch, you are asked to choose a username. A username is required to play the game and track your score. This username is stored in our database, linked to your device identifier, and is publicly visible to all users of the app on the leaderboard. You can change your username at any time from the Home screen. Please do not use a username that reveals personal information you do not want made public.
  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII) — NOT COLLECTED: We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, or password. There is no account creation, login, or sign-in of any kind — only the device identifier and username described above.
  • Your Device's Physical GPS Location — NOT ACCESSED: The Application does not request, track, or access your real-world GPS coordinates or background device location. The only location coordinates processed are the game's pre-configured location data and the virtual coordinate points you voluntarily tap on the guess map.

3. How Gameplay Data and Scores Are Used

LocationGuesser processes gameplay data to support the game modes and the persistent leaderboard:

  • Curated Target Coordinates: Pre-defined latitude and longitude coordinates of locations are stored in our database and used to request panorama imagery from the Google Street View Static API and Maps JavaScript API so you can view visual clues.
  • User Guess Pin Coordinates: When you tap on the map to submit your guess, that coordinate is used locally on your device (via the Haversine formula) to calculate the distance between your guess and the target location and determine your score for that round.
  • Puzzle Images: The image-puzzle feature fetches an image from our database to display as a jigsaw-style puzzle. Solving a puzzle awards points in the same way as a guessing round.
  • Cumulative Score: Points earned from both the location-guessing game and the puzzle feature are added to a single running total score tied to your device identifier and username, stored in our Firebase/Firestore database. This total score is what is displayed on the public leaderboard, ranked against other players.

4. Third-Party Services and APIs

The Application incorporates third-party developer libraries and cloud services to display maps, render street panoramas, store gameplay and leaderboard data, and support app stability. These providers may receive certain technical data (such as your IP address and standard device metadata) as necessary to respond to network requests:

Google Maps Platform

LocationGuesser uses Google Maps Platform APIs, specifically:

  • Google Maps SDK for Android / Maps JavaScript API: Renders the interactive map interface where you view continents, zoom in/out, and tap to place your guess pin, and powers the interactive 360° Street View panorama.
  • Google Street View Static API: Retrieves street-level panoramic images corresponding to the curated location rounds.

These services are governed by Google's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. When requests are made to fetch map tiles or Street View imagery, Google may log standard request information in accordance with their privacy practices.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and Google Maps Platform Terms of Service.

Firebase & Expo Framework

The Application uses Firebase Firestore as a database to store your device identifier, username, cumulative total score, and puzzle image data. This data powers the persistent leaderboard and cumulative scoring features described above.

The Application is also built using the Expo (React Native) ecosystem and may utilize core platform services (such as Expo runtime tooling) for basic error monitoring, dependency resolution, and crash reporting. Any technical diagnostics collected by these frameworks are pseudonymous and used exclusively to diagnose application crashes and ensure compatibility across Android devices.

Learn more: Firebase Privacy and Security and Expo Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention and Storage

Your device identifier, username, and cumulative total score are stored remotely in our Firebase/Firestore database for as long as you have the app installed, so that your progress and leaderboard position persist across sessions. This data remains publicly visible on the leaderboard (username and score only — never your device identifier itself) until removed. We do not sell this data, share it with advertising networks, or use it for marketing purposes. If you would like your data removed, please contact us using the details in Section 9, and provide your username so we can locate and delete the correct record.

6. User Rights and Data Control

Under applicable privacy laws (including GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and Google Play User Data policies), you have the right to control your data.

  • You can change your username at any time from the Home screen; this updates your existing record rather than creating a new one.
  • You can request removal of your device identifier, username, and score data at any time by contacting us using the details in Section 9.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the locally stored device identifier from your device, but does not automatically delete your record from our remote database — if you reinstall the app, a new device identifier will be generated and a new record created. Contact us if you would like your prior record deleted.
  • You can clear local app cache at any time via your Android device settings: Settings → Apps → LocationGuesser → Storage & cache → Clear Storage (note: this will reset your local device identifier, causing a new profile to be created on next launch).

7. Children's Privacy

LocationGuesser is a general audience educational trivia game. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 in certain jurisdictions). The username feature should not be used to enter real names or other identifying information. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate measures.
